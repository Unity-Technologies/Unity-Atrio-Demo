# Neural Training

**Neural Training** trains neural texture compression for your materials from inside the Unity Editor: pick materials in the Neural Training window, train on your machine with the built-in local training server, and get a compact neural material — four block-compressed latent textures and a small network, decoded at shading time by a URP Shader Graph.

This package is **experimental**. For requirements (URP, Python 3.11–3.13), setup and a first run, refer to the [Neural Training documentation](Documentation~/index.md).
